# iPhone basics (iPhone 13 Pro, no home button)

## Navigation
- Home screen: use the `home` tool.
- Open an app: on the home screen, swipe down from the middle of the screen to open Spotlight search, tap the keys of the app name, then tap the app in the results.
- Go back inside an app: tap the "<" back button in the top-left corner. If there is none, swipe right starting at the very left edge of the screen.
- Switch apps: swipe up from the bottom edge and pause, or use Spotlight to open the app again.
- Close a sheet or pop-up: tap "Cancel", "Done", "Close" or "X", or swipe the sheet down.

## Keyboard
- Tap a text field first; wait for the keyboard to appear before typing.
- Letters layout: three rows of letters, then a row with "123", the globe or emoji key, space, and return.
- Capitals: tap the shift key (up arrow, bottom-left of the letter rows) before the letter.
- Numbers and symbols: tap "123"; tap "#+=" for more symbols; tap "ABC" to go back.
- Delete: the key with the "x" in it, right of the bottom letter row.
- After typing, check the field in the screenshot; fix mistakes with delete.
- Autocorrect may change words. If a suggestion bar shows the right word, you can tap it.
- Hide the keyboard: tap outside the field, or tap "Done" if shown.

## Lists and pages
- Scroll with the scroll tool; check the new screenshot to see what moved.
- Pull down at the top of a list to refresh.

## Alerts
- Permission pop-ups (location, notifications, tracking): choose the option that lets the task continue with the least access, e.g. "Allow Once" or "Ask App Not to Track".
- If an unexpected alert blocks the task, read it and ask the user if unsure.
